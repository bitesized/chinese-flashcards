# HSK word list — source

Used only to tag each word with its HSK level (1–6). No Hanzi, Pinyin,
English gloss, or classifier from this source is ever shipped — all
shipped dictionary content comes from CC-CEDICT.

- **Source:** github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
  (commit `7ac65bf1a6387d35f1ade478906172a19311c7f9`), MIT licensed
- **Files used:** `wordlists/exclusive/old/{1..6}.json` — the HSK 2.0
  standard, new words per level (not the cumulative variant)
- **Attribution:** HSK 2.0 word-to-level mapping derived from the
  《汉语水平考试词汇大纲》(Hanban / Chinese Testing International), via the
  drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary project (MIT license).
